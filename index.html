Battle Realms Lookup & Comparison Tool

Unit Counter Lookup & 1v1 Direct Comparison (Zen Edition)

📖 How to Use Counter Lookup (Two-Way Combat Exchange):
• Select Target: Choose a target unit to inspect its armor multipliers and combat exchanges.
• Safe & Dominant Counters (Tier 1): Units that deal high damage (≥125%) to the target while taking low/equal counter-damage (Deals ≥ Takes).
• Effective Counters (Tier 2): Units that deal standard damage (100%) while taking equal or lower counter-damage (Deals ≥ Takes).
• High-Risk Counters (Tier 3): Units that deal effective damage (≥100%) *but* take heavy counter-damage in return (Deals < Takes).
📖 How to Use Weak Resistance Filter:
• Select Damage Type: Choose a damage type (Cutting, Blunt, Piercing, Explosive, or Magical) to list all units that take ≥ 100% damage from that attack type.
• Filter Unit Type: Narrow results by All Units, Melee Only, or Missile Only.
• Sorted by Multiplier: Units are grouped by clan and sorted in ascending order of armor multiplier (from 100% neutral up to highest vulnerability).
📖 How to Use 1v1 Comparison:
• Select Units: Pick Unit A and Unit B from any clan to evaluate a direct head-to-head matchup.
• Armor Exchange Calculation: The tool computes Unit A's primary attack damage against Unit B's armor, and vice versa.
• Matchup Verdict: Instantly displays which unit holds the statistical armor exchange advantage.
